Bootstrap, Leaflet, Express and Socket.IO are third-party packages. Package licenses remain in their installed packages. Do not remove required attributions from redistributed vendor files. The app fetches viewport tiles only; it does not bulk-download maps.

These links identify models. The prices, simplified configurations, tax assumptions and seeded offers in `data.js` are invented classroom fixtures and must not be attributed to manufacturers. The app intentionally avoids claiming current variant availability, official fuel economy, safety ratings or official on-road prices.

Map coordinates are approximate manually selected neighbourhood positions, not coordinates verified by the source. The app tells users to use the source/directions for the entrance.
